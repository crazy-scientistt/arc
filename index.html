How it works

Talk for 90 seconds. Then stop.

Arc listens for people, promises, and drift — the gap between who you said you would be and the week you are having. Everything stays in your browser. No account required for this first version.

What it is not

If the homepage says journal, we already lost. Arc is a plot layer.

Not ChatGPT with memory

Conversation is not the product. Cards, loops, and the film are. Novelty chat dies in a week.

Not a personal CRM

Friends are not leads. Arc only keeps what you said — the promise, not a ping schedule.

Not a habit tracker

No streaks to shame you. The point is sight, not a system you have to maintain.

Not a memoir for later

This is for Tuesday night, while the week can still change.
